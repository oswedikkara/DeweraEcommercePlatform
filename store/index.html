Knowledge & Transparency

Client Advisory & Ordering

Key protocols regarding pure natural heated Ceylon corundum, custody, and acquisitions.

How do I order a gemstone? +
Simply contact us via our acquisition form, WhatsApp, or email deweragems@gmail.com with your selected stone. We will confirm gemstone availability, provide authentic certificate details, and discuss delivery or handover arrangements directly with you.
Do you sell gold jewelry or mountings? +
No. Dewera sells exclusively 100% pure, natural heated loose gemstones. We do not manufacture or sell gold, platinum, or mountings. Every crystal is delivered loose in a certified security cassette with original lab passport, ready for private vault holding or bespoke setting with your independent jeweler.
How does Dewera verify 100% natural heated Ceylon origin? +
Every crystal undergoes microscopic inclusion analysis and Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) confirming traditional thermal silk dissolution alone. We strictly reject beryllium diffusion, cobalt glass-fillings, and surface coatings.
How is delivery and handover arranged? +
We coordinate delivery individually upon purchase based on your preference and location, whether via insured courier delivery or direct in-person handover in Sri Lanka. All logistics are arranged directly with our atelier.
